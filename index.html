Survive & escape

Search shelters for five radio parts. Reflective posts mark dirt tracks, and every shelter has an amber entrance lantern. Check the back rooms for gear and radio components. Open your field map to see the parts you’ve collected and the shelters you’ve entered. Once all five are in your inventory, call for extraction from the map.

Switch the flashlight off to recharge it. Walk quietly behind a zombie to avoid its field of view. Gunshots can alert nearby enemies, and a flare fired away from your route can draw them off. A single hit is fatal without armor.

Ammo is available both outside and inside. Search shelters for weapons, armor, upgrades, and radio parts. Walk close to collect ammo, upgrades, and parts. Press the equip control to take a weapon or better armor.
