# Asset Credits
The Last Light weapons, gloves, flashlight, wreck, zombie rigs, and geometry are authored for this project. The Blender source is in `scripts/author-assets.py`. The three zombie rigs and their animation clips are original.

- Forest ground by Charlotte Baglioni, [Forest Ground 06](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_06), CC0.
- Cedar bark by Charlotte Baglioni, [Chinese Cedar Bark](https://polyhaven.com/a/chinese_cedar_bark), CC0.
- Boulder material by Dimitrios Savva and Rico Cilliers, [Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), CC0.

The historical alpha bundles Student Zombie and OfficeWorker assets from [EverydayNPCs Vol.1 Free Sample v1.4](https://sh4rkdev.itch.io/everyday-npcs-free-sample) by Sh4rkDev. These legacy binary chunks are excluded from the rebuild's runtime output. Do not redistribute the original archives as standalone content.
Gunshots: Gunshot Sounds (CC0), https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
Reloads: Gun Reload Sounds (CC0), https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
Zombie voices: Zombies Sound Pack (CC0), https://opengameart.org/content/zombies-sound-pack
Zombie voices: Zombie Noises and Moans (CC0), https://opengameart.org/content/zombie-noises-and-moans
