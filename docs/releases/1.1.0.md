# Release 1.1.0 ("Dead Forest")

**Rollback target:** `v1.0.17` (commit `13f4e84`).

## Added
- **Independent environment dressing (`src/dressing.js`, `src/kit.js`):**
  - 5 rock shapes (round, flat slab, craggy spire, split, mossy) across pebble clusters, road-shoulder pebbles, low forest stones, and rare colliding boulders (`sy > 1.25`, max 2 per chunk).
  - Road-shoulder gravel vertex-color grit (`turf()` in `src/presentation.js`) and pebble scatter.
  - Fallen decorative logs and passable road-verge log barriers (`logBarrier`, 4.4–5.8 m) with 3 axis-aligned collision boxes and guaranteed walkable detours.
  - Dead standing trees (snags, ~8% of trees) reusing existing trunks with collapsed crowns and omitted canopy shade.
  - Grass tufts (6 tris) and thorn/dead shrubs (12 tris) as instanced cover, respecting quality tiers 1–3.
  - Animal carcasses (deer, grizzly bear, fox, rabbit in 2 variants each) with dark ground stains, skulls, and scattered bones.
  - Stylized clothed human remains (slumped against trees or lying down) with backpacks, no exposed anatomy or loot.
  - Abandoned roadside vehicles (`sedan`, `pickup`, `van` in 4 weathered hues) and campsite clutter (`moto`, `motoFallen`, `stump`, `pack`, `bones`).
- **Atmosphere & audio (`src/ambient-life.js`, `src/audio.js`):**
  - Single-draw-call view-space instanced shader particle layer (`FIREFLIES = 26`, `FLIES = 22`) for seeded firefly pockets and flashlight/flare-revealed carcass flies, with zero real-time lights or shadow casters.
  - Positional carcass fly buzz (`setBuzz`) and rare distant crow caw (`caw`) on the ambience bus, kept below threat voices.
- **Pop-in elimination & diagnostics (`src/presentation.js`, `src/game.js`):**
  - Time-sliced chunk streaming (1 incremental chunk build per frame), heading-biased prefetch (`22 m` ahead), and idle 5×5 outer-ring world-data warm-up.
  - Menu shader pre-warm (`warmGroup`) for both `surfaces.remains` and instanced `surfaces.fern`.
  - Smooth distance opacity fades near the `34 m` loot and `68 m` signal-flare sprite cutoffs.
  - `?dressing=off` live toggle, `?debug&scenario=dressing` inspection scenario, and `chunk <maxMs> ms` telemetry in `?perf=1` and `render_game_to_text()`.
- **Automated coverage (`test/dressing.test.js`, `scripts/audit-world.mjs`):**
  - Golden snapshot regression test against 1.0.17 (`test/fixtures/world-1.0.17.json`), determinism, triangle/collider/draw-call budgets, clearances, barrier BFS navigability, 3.5 m raycast padding invariant, and frequency bounds.

## Changed
- **Signal flare brightness (`src/presentation.js` `updateSignals` only):**
  - Landed intensity `43 × p` -> `69 × p`; landed reach `1.7 + 7 × p` -> `2.4 + 9.8 × p`; in-flight intensity `19 × p` -> `30 × p`; in-flight reach `1.7 + 3 × p` -> `2.4 + 4.2 × p`; halo scale `.3 + 1.2 × p` -> `.4 + 1.7 × p` (in-flight `.59` -> `.72`). Reduced Flashes still scales by `0.64`.
- **Version metadata:** bumped `BUILD` (`src/simulation.js`), `package.json`, and `package-lock.json` from `1.0.17` to `1.1.0`.

## Left untouched
- Simulation combat, damage, weapon stats, upgrade caps, AI perception, `signalFlarePower` flare distraction, horde curves, radio odds/placement, district layouts, and `localStorage` schemas (`zomboidz.settings.v1`, `zomboidz.scores.v1`).
- Existing chunk RNG stream (`rng` in `world.js`): every 1.0.17 seed keeps identical trees, buildings, radio placement, loot IDs, and entrance candidates.
