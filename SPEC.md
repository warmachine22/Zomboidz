# Zomboidz — The Last Light: v0.8
Meta Quest WebXR / tablet browser experimental single-life survival game. Last as long as possible in a dark, dense forest with an aimable/rechargeable flashlight and pistol. Headshots immediately kill. Body hits require repeated fire. Zombies move slowly but increase in number from ~18 to a target of 64, with an adaptive headset performance ceiling. Upgrades and new weapons appear in dim ground chests. A fatal zombie attack ends the run and shows a short third-person reconstruction.
v0.8 changes: shake-to-charge uses quick head-relative translation OR wrist rotation sampled over time (does not require unrealistically large per-frame reversal); the wristwatch shows charging status; flashlight pickups meaningfully improve capacity (up to 220) and endurance (lower discharge floor). Hit detection follows the actual animated zombie Head bone, and the laser dot changes from red to gold on a registered head hit. On death, the skinned zombie rig remains parented to the world; hips/limbs collapse and settle close to the ground, preventing floating or teleporting corpses.
Validated: simulated rapid shake charges, passive movement doesn't, head-aimed pistol shot registers instant kill, fallen corpse grounds at approximately ground level, desktop and touch browser smoke tests. Quest headset hardware not tested.
